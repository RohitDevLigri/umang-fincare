Home Loan

Turn Your Dream Home Into Reality With Affordable EMIs

Compare housing loan offers across leading banks and HFCs for ready-to-move flats, under-construction properties, plot construction, and balance transfers.

Key Features

  • High Loan-to-Value (LTV) Funding: Finance up to 75%–90% of the registered property value depending on loan slab and lender policy.
  • Flexible Repayment Tenure: Extended repayment period of up to 30 years to keep monthly EMIs comfortable.
  • Zero Prepayment Penalty on Floating Rates: Part-prepay or foreclose floating-rate home loans without extra charges as per RBI guidelines.
  • Balance Transfer & Top-Up Facility: Shift your existing high-interest home loan to a lower ROI lender with additional top-up funds.
  • Tax Benefits Under Income Tax Act: Claim deductions on principal repayment (Section 80C) and interest paid (Section 24b) as applicable.

Documents Required

  • KYC Documents: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID along with current address proof.
  • Income Proof (Salaried): Latest 3 months' salary slips, Form 16, and last 2 years' Income Tax Returns (ITR).
  • Income Proof (Self-Employed): Last 2–3 years' ITR with computation of income, Balance Sheet, and Profit & Loss account.
  • Bank Statements: Last 6 months' active salary or primary business current/savings account statements.
  • Property Papers: Allotment letter, Sale Agreement, approved building plan, and complete title chain documents.
