Vehicle Loan

Drive Home Your New or Pre-Owned Vehicle With Ease

Compare auto finance options for new cars, certified used cars, electric vehicles, commuter bikes, and superbikes across India.

Key Features

  • Up to 90%–100% On-Road Funding: Minimal down-payment options on select new cars, EVs, and two-wheelers.
  • New, Pre-Owned & EV Coverage: Flexible financing across petrol, diesel, CNG, hybrid, and electric models.
  • Comfortable Tenure Options: Repay smoothly over up to 7 years for cars and up to 3–5 years for two-wheelers.
  • Quick Sanction & Dealer Delivery: Fast credit approval and direct payment to authorized dealerships for timely delivery.
  • Fixed & Transparent EMIs: Predictable monthly installments with clear foreclosure and hypothecation terms.

Documents Required

  • Identity & Age Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Driving Licence.
  • Residence Proof: Aadhaar Card, electricity/gas bill, passport, or registered rental agreement.
  • Income Proof: Latest 3 months' salary slips & Form 16 (salaried) or last 2 years' ITR (self-employed).
  • Bank Statements: Last 6 months' primary bank account statement showing regular income or business cash flow.
  • Vehicle Quotation / RC Copy: Dealer proforma invoice (for new vehicle) or RC & valuation report (for pre-owned vehicle).
