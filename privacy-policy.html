Who We Are & Our Mission

Umang FinCare is a pan-India loan and insurance facilitator built on a simple commitment: Har Zarurat Mein Aapke Saath. Whether a family is purchasing its first home, funding higher education, expanding a small business, or protecting a two-wheeler or car on Indian roads, navigating dozens of banks, NBFCs, and insurance policies can feel overwhelming. We bridge that gap by bringing clear, side-by-side options and honest human guidance under one roof.

Our mission is to make credit and insurance accessible, transparent, and stress-free for salaried professionals, self-employed entrepreneurs, and households across India. Instead of pushing one-size-fits-all products, we listen carefully to your financial goals, help verify documentation upfront, and coordinate smoothly with reputable lenders and insurers from initial enquiry through to final approval or policy issuance.
