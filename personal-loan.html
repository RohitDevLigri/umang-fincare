Personal Loan

Instant Unsecured Funds When Life Needs Support

Meet medical emergencies, wedding expenses, home renovation, travel, or debt consolidation without pledging any collateral.

Key Features

  • 100% Collateral-Free Financing: No security, guarantor, or asset mortgage required for loan sanction.
  • Fast Digital Approval & Disbursal: Minimal paperwork with quick sanction and direct bank account credit.
  • Flexible Usage Freedom: Use funds for weddings, medical care, higher education, travel, or urgent household needs.
  • Customizable Repayment Tenure: Choose a repayment schedule from 12 months up to 5 years to match your monthly budget.
  • Balance Transfer & Top-Up Options: Consolidate existing high-cost EMIs into one manageable monthly installment.

Documents Required

  • Identity Proof: PAN Card (mandatory) along with Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Driving Licence.
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, utility bill, passport, or registered rent agreement.
  • Latest Salary Slips: Last 3 months' salary slips (for salaried applicants) or latest ITR (for self-employed).
  • Bank Account Statements: Last 3–6 months' primary salary or operational bank statement showing regular credits.
  • Employment / Business Proof: Official employee ID card, appointment letter, or business registration proof.
